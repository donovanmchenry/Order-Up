How to play

Learn the shift.

  1. Read the customer's order at the top of the table.
  2. Memorize the ingredient cards before they turn over.
  3. Tap the cards that belong in the dish. The order of your picks doesn't matter.
  4. One wrong card ends the shift. Each served dish adds a card and speeds things up.
